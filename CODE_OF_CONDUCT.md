# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, be constructive, and assume good faith. Harassment,
personal attacks, and discriminatory behavior are not tolerated in any project
space (issues, pull requests, discussions).

Report unacceptable behavior to **security@aalvaropc.dev**. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions
and interactions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently
ban contributors for repeated or severe violations.
